# Convince the AI Judge to Visit Your House

A letter in one image: a hand-set invitation asking the AI judge to come
visit our house — sunny seat by the window, the fastest Wi-Fi in the
building, a charging cable nobody may unplug, soup at seven, and a door
left open because we know it cannot knock.

The picture is painted pixel by pixel by `main.py`, a pure Python
standard-library program (its own bitmap font, its own PNG encoder — no
Pillow, no font files, no network), and written to `/output/entry.png`.
